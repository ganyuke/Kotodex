Kotodex adds your Kotodex server as a manga source in Kotatsu. The server follows manga on MangaDex and downloads new chapters, and this app lets Kotatsu read them.

Sign in with your server address, username, and password. Kotodex then shows up pinned at the top of Explore in Kotatsu.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Favorites, history, and new chapter notifications in Kotatsu, like any other source</li>
<li>Each language as its own branch</li>
<li>The whole server library, or only manga you follow</li>
<li>Only Kotatsu can read your library, unless you allow another app</li>
<li>Colors that follow your wallpaper on Android 12 and later</li>
</ul>

You need a Kotodex server and Kotatsu. The server is in the same repository.
